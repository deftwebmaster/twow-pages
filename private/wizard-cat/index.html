Meet the Characters

Wizard Cat

Grayson

Guardian of the threshold between worlds, Wizard Cat is a master of ancient magic and advanced napping techniques. His magnificent floof is often mistaken for excess weight by those who don't understand that raw magical power requires substantial insulation. When not defending the realm from shadows, he can be found exactly three inches behind his favorite person at all times. His hobbies include supervising, judging, and being right where you're about to step.

Magic Girl

Amy

Amy—wait, we mean Magic Girl—is a creative badass whose golden fire magic is rivaled only by her ability to craft entire miniature worlds with her bare hands. Some say she discovered her powers while making tiny furniture; others say the tiny furniture was the first spell she ever cast. When she's not battling darkness alongside her fluffy companion, she enjoys relaxation herbs and proving that real magic is making something from nothing.
